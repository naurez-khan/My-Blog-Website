About

I'm Naurez — a 19 year old student at the University of Engineering and Technology, Lahore. This site is where I'm keeping notes as I get into the habit of writing in public: things I'm learning, noticing, and working through, written down before I forget them.

Most of it won't be polished. That's kind of the point — a place for half-finished thoughts instead of only the ones I'm sure about.

If something here is useful, wrong, or worth arguing about, I'd like to hear it — see the contact page for the best way to reach me.
